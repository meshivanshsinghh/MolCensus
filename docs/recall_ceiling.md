# Recall ceiling for the six course papers

Date: 2026-11-05. Task item 4 (recall-ceiling labels). No labels were set by hand.

This sorts the 146 course molecules that no reader found (MolScribe, MolSight or MolGlyph on the 237
pipeline crops, section 6 of `docs/aws_run_2026-11-04.md`) into five buckets:

| Bucket | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Drawn in full and cropped, but misread (reading loss) |
| 2 | Drawn in full, but not cropped (detection loss) |
| 3 | Only through an R-group table, placeholder or linker box (table loss) |
| 4 | Not in the paper's figures |
| 5 | Unresolved |

A molecule stays unresolved unless the evidence supports a bucket. This page gives per-paper totals only.
The per-molecule labels are in `results/aws/scored/recall_ceiling.csv` (gitignored), with columns
`paper_id`, `gt_name`, `bucket`, `bucket_name`, `evidence_layer`, `reason` (one line), `layer2_bucket`,
`claude` and `gemini`.

Rerun everything with one command from the repo root:

```bash
python scripts/analysis/ceiling_run.py              # finished steps are reused from outputs/ceiling/
python scripts/analysis/ceiling_run.py --no-gemini  # no new Gemini calls; cached answers are still used
```

A Gemini call needs `VERTEX_PROJECT` and `VERTEX_SERVICE_ACCOUNT` in `.env` or the shell: a Google Cloud
project with Vertex AI, and a service account that your gcloud login can impersonate. The Claude answers cannot be regenerated by a script. They are read from
`outputs/ceiling/judges/claude/`. To redo a step, delete its file in `outputs/ceiling/`.

## Summary

- **Almost every miss is table loss.** 138 of the 146 missed molecules (95%) exist in the paper only as
  a row of an R-group table, as a scaffold with a placeholder, or through a linker box. A crop-by-crop
  reader cannot output them, however well it reads.
- **2 are reading loss.** Both are paper 16 macrocycles drawn over a colored heatmap.
- **None ended up as detection loss or missing from the figures.** A second detection pass at a much
  lower threshold found nothing new. The judges' one detection-loss answer (paper 6: drawn in full, but
  split across two crops) contradicts layer 2, so that molecule stays unresolved.
- **6 stay unresolved.** In 5 of them the automatic evidence and both judges disagree. Both judges say 4
  of those 5 are drawn in full and cropped, and say the fifth is split across two crops. In the 6th the
  two judges disagree.
- **The readers are near the ceiling.** Of the 219 molecules, 75 to 81 (0.342 to 0.370) are drawn in
  full. The readers together found 73 (0.333). Better reading can add at most 2 to 8 molecules. Paper-level
  results need readers that understand tables.

## How the labels were reached

### Layer 1: what the ground truth records

The ground-truth CSV has three columns: paper, molecule name, SMILES. It records no page, figure or table
and no scaffold or R-group origin. The names are compound labels. For the 146 missed molecules:
99 are plain numbers, 38 are a number and a letter (a compound series), 8 are names or codes, and 1 is
"unnamed compound N in Scheme M". Layer 1 assigns no bucket. It supplies the label to look for. Each label
that is not a plain number (47 of the missed) was searched for in the PDF text layer, as a whole word.
45 were found on at least one page. The judges were told those pages.

### Layer 2: automatic evidence, no judgement

| Test | What counts as positive evidence | Result for the 146 |
|---|---|---|
| a. Detection (bucket 2) | MolDetV2 reruns on the same page images at confidence 0.05 (the pipeline uses 0.4). A box below 0.4 that lies at most 50% inside a pipeline crop is a new crop. MolScribe reads it on the Mac. It counts if the reading matches a missed molecule by InChIKey or canonical SMILES. | 287 boxes. The 237 at 0.4 or more are the pipeline crops. 38 are new (paper 2: 19, paper 4: 10, paper 19: 9). **None** reads as a missed molecule. |
| b. Tables (bucket 3) | A reading with `*` atoms (224 of the 705 valid readings, from 155 crops) whose core, the reading without its `*` atoms, has at least 8 atoms and is an exact substructure of the missed molecule. The molecule may branch off that core only where the `*` atoms were, with no extra bonds between core atoms. Time limit: 30 s per molecule. | 139 match, 7 do not, 0 time out. |
| c. Reading (bucket 1) | A pipeline crop's reading, with no `*` atoms and not itself a ground-truth molecule, has Tanimoto ≥ 0.8 to the missed molecule. The missed molecule must also be that reading's single closest ground-truth molecule in the paper, so a correct reading of a sibling compound does not count. | 3 at 0.8 or more. |

Rules: test c contradicts tests a and b, so a molecule with c and either a or b goes to layer 3. Test a
outranks b, because a full drawing outranks a table, but a never fired. Result: 138 molecules get bucket 3
from test b, 2 get bucket 1 from test c, 1 has conflicting evidence (b and c) and 5 have none.

Close to the 0.8 line of test c: 4 molecules have their best reading in [0.75, 0.80) and 1 in
[0.80, 0.85). Moving the line by 0.05 would change at most 4 labels.

The layer 2 code is in `scripts/analysis/ceiling_evidence.py`. The new crops are in
`outputs/ceiling/crops_lowthr/`. The pipeline crops were not touched.

### Layer 3: two AI judges, tested blind first

**Packets.** All 219 molecules are shuffled within each paper and given random IDs (M100 to M999). They
are split into 25 packets of at most 10, one paper per packet. Both judges got the same packets. A packet
holds:
- the paper's pages, at most 1568 px on the long side, with the pipeline crops drawn as labelled red
  boxes. Pages shown: every page with a pipeline crop, a low-threshold box or a label hit in the text.
  That is 74 pages: paper 1: 13 of 13, paper 2: 10 of 19, paper 4: 13 of 16, paper 6: 17 of 25,
  paper 16: 3 of 13, paper 19: 18 of 18.
- one sheet of RDKit drawings of the packet's molecules, under their IDs. No SMILES appears in any prompt.
- the same written instructions (in `ceiling_judges.py`).

The instructions ask, for each molecule, for the first true answer in this order:
- A: drawn in full, inside one red box.
- B: drawn in full, but no box holds the whole drawing.
- C: only through a scaffold with R-groups and a table, a placeholder, a linker box or separate fragments.
- D: not drawn on any shown page.
- E: cannot tell.

A maps to bucket 1, B to 2, C to 3 and D to 4.

**Known answers.** 213 of the 219 have a known answer. For the 73 molecules a reader found, it is A.
For each layer 2 positive, it is the matching answer: test b gives C, test c gives A. Neither judge saw
the key (`outputs/ceiling/judges/key.csv`).

**Judges.**
- **Gemini:** `gemini-3.5-flash` on Vertex AI, signed in through `scripts/aws/molcensus_vertex.py`
  (the gcloud login impersonates the service account). One call per packet, one at a time, JSON reply, default temperature. That was 25
  calls, 440,202 input tokens and 15,762 output tokens. Every call returned an answer for every ID.
- **Claude:** Claude Code (Claude Opus 5.5) in the session that wrote this code. It viewed the same 74
  page images and 25 sheets. It also opened 4 pipeline crops at full size and one enlarged part of a page,
  which Gemini did not get. Its answers were written to files before it opened the key. Their SHA-256 at
  2026-11-05T05:53:33Z was `75a939594871bbce58423f8b2a549a07994e35595af496c3e22e9bf946cd7a31`
  (`outputs/ceiling/judges/claude_answers_sha256_before_key.txt`). It was not fully blind: it wrote the
  layer 2 code, saw the layer 2 totals, and saw how many missed molecules each scaffold crop matched. It
  did not see which molecules were found or any per-molecule layer 2 result.

**Acceptance.** A layer 3 label is accepted only for a molecule with no layer 2 bucket. Both judges must
have scored at least 90% on the known cases, and both must give the same answer, A to D.

**One rule added to the brief.** Suppose both trusted judges give the same answer, and it contradicts a
layer 2 label. Then that layer 2 label is withdrawn and the molecule becomes unresolved. The judges'
answer is not used in its place. The rule can only remove a label. It fired 5 times. The strict rule,
without it, would give 3 in bucket 1, 142 in bucket 3 and 1 unresolved. The CSV keeps `layer2_bucket`, so
either version can be rebuilt.

## Limits

- **Layer 2b reads the readers' `*` atoms as R-groups.** It cannot tell an R-group from an abbreviation
  the reader could not expand. The judges caught 4 such cases, but a missed case where both judges also
  answered C would stay labelled table loss. Of the 134 test b labels that stand, both judges answered C
  for 132. For the other 2, one judge answered A or E.
- **Answer D means "not on the shown pages".** Pages without any detector box at 0.05 and without a label
  hit were not shown. No molecule got D, so this did not decide any label.
- **The judges did not see exactly the same images.** Claude opened 4 crops at full size and one
  enlarged region. It did so to check abbreviations and a 3D graphic. Gemini saw only the packet images.
- **Gemini is not deterministic.** A rerun with `outputs/ceiling/judges/gemini/` deleted would give
  slightly different answers. The cached answers are the ones used here.
- **Test 2a found nothing.** That is evidence, not proof, that detection loses no complete drawings in
  these papers. MolDetV2 at 0.05 can still miss a drawing, and MolScribe can misread a new crop.
